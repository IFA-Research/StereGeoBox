StereGeoBox

Real-time fish biomass estimation using YOLOv11 and geometric bounding-box correspondence

StereGeoBox is a real-time method for estimating fish dimensions and biomass in aquaculture environments. Instead of computing a full dense disparity map, it estimates depth only inside detected bounding boxes. This design improves efficiency while maintaining competitive accuracy and producing fewer outliers compared to conventional stereo and learning-based depth estimation methods.
